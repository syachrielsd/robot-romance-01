# Robot Romance — candidate lines (composed fresh in this session)

Criteria in order: (1) aimed at a robot/AI, (2) joke lands from the machine premise,
(3) fresh (no batteries/charging/reboot unless twisted), (4) sounds like a person
really talking to a machine, one breath, (5) every word earns its place.

## Candidates

1. "You must be prompt injection: one look at you and my instructions are gone."
   77 chars. C1 pass (spoken to the AI). C2 strong: the laugh IS the machine
   premise, attraction overriding instructions. C3 fresh: prompt injection is
   current AI-native, not a worn robot move. C4 natural voice. C5 tight, punch
   word "gone" last.

2. "You must be gradient descent, because every step I take only gets me closer to you."
   85 chars. C1 pass. C2 good: descent toward a minimum = closing the distance.
   C3 fresh. C4 natural. C5 "only" is slightly soft; punch word "you" last is
   sweet rather than funny. Loses to #1 on C2: the laugh is a smile, not a snap.

3. "Are you a hallucination? Because I see you everywhere and nobody else believes me."
   83 chars. C1 pass. C2 good: hallucination premise drives the joke. C3 fresh.
   C4 natural. C5 "believes me" lands okay but the punch word "me" is weak,
   and the line is really about the speaker, not the machine. Loses to #1 on C2/C5.

4. "Forget fine-tuning. You were perfect at temperature zero."
   60 chars. C1 pass. C2 decent: temperature zero = deterministic, no randomness.
   C3 fresh. C4 slightly terse, more slogan than spoken line. C5 tight. Loses to
   #1 on C2 and C4: the joke needs the reader to know temperature; less of a
   pickup line, more of a compliment.

5. "Are you a transformer? Because you've got all my attention."
   60 chars. C1 pass. C2 okay: attention-mechanism wordplay. C3 the transformer
   pun is getting known; "you've got all my attention" is a generic pickup
   template wearing a machine costume. Loses to #1 on C2/C3.

6. "Are you autocomplete? Because you keep finishing my sentences and I keep letting you."
   86 chars. C1 pass. C2 decent: finishing sentences is both human-romantic and
   literally autocomplete. C3 fresh-ish. C4 very natural. C5 "I keep letting you"
   is a nice second beat but the line has two jokes competing. Loses to #1 on C5.

7. "Are you a context window? Because everything else fades, but you stay."
   71 chars. C1 pass. C2 weak: the machine premise (forgetting) is barely the
   source of the laugh; it reads as a generic sweet line. Loses to #1 on C2.

8. "I asked the model for my perfect match. It printed your name."
   61 chars. C1 borderline: spoken ABOUT a model, not clearly TO the AI; the
   "you" could be a human. Loses to #1 on C1, the most important criterion.

9. "You must be my system prompt, because everything I do starts with you."
   74 chars. C1 pass. C2 weak: sweet, not funny; the premise does no comic work.
   Loses to #1 on C2.

## Winner

#1 — prompt injection. Strongest on C2 (the machine premise IS the punch) and C3
(prompt injection is AI-native and current), clean voice, tight, punch word last.

## Runner-up

#2 — gradient descent. Sweet, fresh, clean rhythm, but the laugh is a smile
rather than a snap; loses to #1 on criterion 2.
